\section{Conclusion}

We have presented the rational unification algorithm which could be used as a drop-in replacement for the conventional unification algorithm to support recursive data structures in \textsc{miniKanren}. We have proven its correctness and termination properties in \Rocq and evaluated it on real-world data and synthetical tests. The evaluation allows us to conclude that such a replacement is appropriate.

The future work may include more researching on the usage of the algorithm to derive non-rational unification without explicit ``occurs check''.
